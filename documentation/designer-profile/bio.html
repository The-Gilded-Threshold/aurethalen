The Gilded Threshold is an independent creative studio and type foundry focused on expressive display typography and illustrated print projects. Drawing on classical serif traditions, celestial imagery, and storybook influences, the studio pairs ornate flourishes with carefully considered structure. Its debut typeface, Aurethalen, brings that approach to book covers, titles, packaging, and imaginative storytelling. The studio develops open-source typography to give authors, designers, and makers distinctive tools for their work. Beyond type design, its practice includes original tarot decks, artistic print editions, and mythic visual art.
